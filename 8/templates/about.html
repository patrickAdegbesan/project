Simulation Architecture

The system models a linear chain of N nodes (representing base stations or routers in a Nigerian urban 4G deployment). Packets are generated at the ingress node according to a Poisson process with rate λ packets/second, traverse each node's finite FIFO buffer, and are served with exponentially distributed service times at rate μ packets/second. After service at each node, packets are forwarded to the next node in the chain.

The system is validated against the closed-form M/M/1 queuing model. With λ = 0.8 and μ = 1.0 the theoretical mean sojourn time is E[T] = 1 / (μ − λ) = 5.0 s. The simulation produces results within 10 % of this theoretical value, confirming implementation correctness.

M/M/1 Key Formulae
  • Server utilisation: ρ = λ / μ
  • Mean sojourn time: E[T] = 1 / (μ − λ)
  • Mean queue length: E[L] = ρ / (1 − ρ)
  • Mean waiting time: E[W] = ρ / (μ − λ)
