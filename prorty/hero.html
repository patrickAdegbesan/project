Frequently asked questions

Everything buyers ask us.

Land size, title, payments and how the mall is run once it's built — the answers we give most often.

Land & building

How big is the total land and each shop?

The entire Rehoboth-Winners Shopping Mall sits on 500 SQM of land, divided into 8 equal commercial slots of 62.5 SQM each. The mall is two-storey, so every slot gives you two shops — one upstairs and one downstairs — 16 shops in total.

Is it up and down or bungalow?

It's up and down — a two-storey building. Each 62.5 SQM slot covers two shops, one on the ground floor and one on the first floor, with toilet space included. Want just one shop? Pair with someone and split the payment.

Is there parking and other facilities?

Yes. The layout includes customer parking, 24/7 security, common area lighting, waste disposal, and drainage.

Location & access

Where exactly is the mall?

Rehoboth-Winners Shopping Mall sits inside Bluestone Garden City, Ire Estate, Mowe — Obafemi Owode LGA, Ogun State. It faces the estate road.

How much passing traffic will my shop see?

Ire Estate is the gateway to the estates behind it — residents of all of them pass through Ire Estate to get home. That daily flow of people past your shopfront is what makes this location work for retail.

How will customers know the mall is there?

A billboard signpost goes up on the main road advertising the mall and everything on sale, directing main-road traffic into the estate.

Purchase & developer

Who is selling the shop slots?

Rehoboth-Winners Shopping Mall slots are being sold by Ire Estate. Ire Estate is the developer and allocation authority.

Do I have to deal directly with Ogun State Government?

No. As a buyer, you have no direct dealings with Ogun State Government. Ire Estate will handle all payments, levies, and C of O processing on behalf of all 8 owners.

Title & documentation

What documents will I receive?

You will receive an Allocation Letter, Purchase Receipt, Contract Agreement, and Deed of Assignment from Ire Estate. Ire Estate will also process and deliver the C of O for the property.

What is the title on the land?

Ire Estate holds the master title for the 500 SQM from Ogun State. Each buyer gets a Deed of Assignment for their 62.5 SQM portion, backed by the master C of O.

Payment & fees

What payment plans are available?

Payment per slot is ₦7.5m. A minimum of ₦5m secures your slot, and the balance is completed within 2 months.

To talk through your own schedule, call the Rehoboth Mall Management team — 0803 334 6916 or 0907 719 5867.

How much does it cost to build?

Building costs are not fixed, as they are influenced by prevailing market prices for construction materials and labour. To ensure fair and accurate pricing, a detailed quotation will be provided based on current market rates at the time construction is scheduled.

What fees am I responsible for?

Only the Monthly Mall Service Charge for security, cleaning, and common maintenance, paid to Ire Estate / Mall Management. Ire Estate will also collect and remit the Annual Tenement Rate to Obafemi Owode LGA on behalf of owners.

Use & management

Can I merge 2 shops together?

Yes. You can merge 2 shops together if both slots are beside each other. No approval is required.

What kind of business can I operate?

Any approved commercial business: retail, boutique, pharmacy, office, restaurant, bank, etc. Subject to Rehoboth Mall rules.

Can I sell or rent my shop later?

Yes. But the Manager must be aware. No payment charges or transfer fees are required.

When can I start building / fitting out my shop?

Immediately after allocation. The building budget cost will be gotten and the bill will be split among the 8 slot owners to proceed with the building stage.

Who manages the mall after sales?

Ire Estate / Rehoboth Mall Management Team will manage security, cleaning, maintenance, and run the "Rehoboth Mall Owners Association" with all 8 owners.
