Frequently Asked Questions

How do I assign a complaint to a staff handler?
Open the complaint from All Complaints, click the Assign button on the right, then select a handler from the dropdown. Only users with the "Handler" or "Administrator" role appear in the list. You can also use Bulk Assign from the complaints table to assign multiple at once.
How do I change a complaint's status?
Open the complaint detail page and click Update Status in the top-right action panel. You can move it to In Progress, Under Review, Resolved, or Closed. The complainant receives an email notification on every status change (if notifications are enabled in Settings).
A student says they can't log in — how do I help?
Go to Users, search for the student by name or matric number, open their profile, and click Reset Password. This sends a password-reset link to their registered email. If their account is suspended, click Activate Account first.
How do I export the complaints report to Excel?
From Reports & Analytics, select your date range and filters, then click the Export button in the top-right. You can export as CSV or PDF. From All Complaints, the Export button exports only the currently filtered view.
How do I put the portal in maintenance mode?
Go to Settings → General → Portal Status and toggle Portal Active off. Users will see a maintenance message. Post an Announcement beforehand so users know when to expect downtime. Admins can still log in during maintenance mode.
How are complaints auto-assigned to departments?
When Auto-assign to department is enabled in Settings → Complaint Rules, the system uses the complaint's Category to route it to the default handler for that category. You can set default handlers per category in Users → Role Assignments.
