The Project

Rehoboth-Winners Shopping Mall is a proposed sixteen-shop commercial development at Bluestone Garden City — designed as a one-stop functional mall serving the estate and its surrounding communities.

The mall sits on a 500 sqm commercial plot in a prime position facing the Bluestone Garden City estate road, giving every shop direct visibility to estate traffic. The building comprises eight shops on the ground floor and eight above, with toilet facilities, borehole and water storage, full electrical and plumbing infrastructure, drainage, external painting and a parking lot.

Bluestone Garden City is a secured, fast-growing community built to VGC standard, with a minimum of eight other estates inside it — a ready and growing customer base for every business in the mall.

The Four Project Stages

  1. Securing the land

    Acquisition of the 500 sqm commercial plot with every legal documentation — survey, deeds and global C of O.

  2. Documentation & design

    Individual legal documentation per slot; architectural design; electrical design and budget cost; mechanical building cost; cost of materials; professional, legal and consultant fees.

  3. Construction to roof level

    Building from scratch to roof level with all plumbing and electrical piping/wiring in place; plastering of the entire building; windows, burglary bars and roll-shutter doors fitted; borehole, tank scaffold, soakaway and all drainage systems; external painting and parking lot.

  4. Individual finishing

    Tiling, internal painting, electrical and plumbing fittings, ceiling works, meter and overhead tank — completed by each owner to their own taste and standard.
