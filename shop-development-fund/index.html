About the Project

Building Value, Together

The Shop Development Fund was created to finance the development of a commercial shop project. The objective is straightforward: pool contributions from investors and partners to complete the development, then keep every contributor informed as the project moves forward.

Rather than a complicated onboarding process, we have kept things simple. Contributions are made through a dedicated, secure payment link — no website registration, no account creation, no paperwork to get started.

  • Clearly defined project purpose and objectives
  • Contributions of any size are welcome
  • Payments confirmed instantly by Paystack
  • Progress updates published on this page
  • Direct line to the project team for enquiries
