About the Fund

The Shop Development Fund exists for one purpose: to finance the development of the shop project and to give every contributor a clear record of their payment.

Rather than asking contributors to register on a website or complete paperwork, the fund operates through a single dedicated payment page. You choose the amount, enter your name and email address, and pay by card, bank transfer or USSD. Paystack issues a receipt immediately, and the project team reconciles all contributions against the fund's records.

Progress on the development — including milestones reached and photographs of work completed — is published on this page under Updates, so contributors can follow the project without needing to log in anywhere.

03

How to Contribute

  1. Open the payment page

    Follow the Make a Payment button, or type paystack.shop/pay/shop-development-fund into your browser. The page works on any phone or computer.

  2. Enter your details and amount

    Provide your full name, email address and the amount you wish to contribute, then choose card, bank transfer or USSD.

  3. Keep your receipt

    Paystack emails your receipt at once. If you do not receive it within a few minutes, check your spam folder or contact the project team with the approximate time of payment.
